# One topic, 30 angles

Take one narrow topic and ask it 30 different questions. Each answer is a
post (often a list of three), so one topic fills a month. When the month is
done, take the same 30 angles with a narrower or neighbouring topic, or run
them again with new answers.

How to use it with the user:

1. Agree the topic, narrow and with a reader in mind (see the idea builder in
   `postemi-writing`): not "gardening" but "growing vegetables on a small
   balcony for people new to it".
2. Go through the angles, fill each question with the topic, and answer it
   from the user's own experience or a named source. Skip angles the user has
   nothing true to say about; never fill one with invented facts.
3. Give each post a hook (`postemi-writing`, or a hook template from
   `postemi-templates`) and a format (short post, thread, carousel, video).
4. Spread them over the month and schedule them with Postemi
   (`postemi-growth`, section 5).

Mark each angle in the post log, so the review shows which angles this
audience likes.

## Worked example

Topic: growing vegetables on a small balcony, for people new to it.

| Day | Angle | Hook | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mistakes | 3 balcony garden mistakes that kill seedlings in week one | short post |
| 2 | Tools | The only 4 tools you need for a balcony vegetable patch | carousel |
| 3 | Stories | Last spring I grew 9 kilos of tomatoes on a 2 square metre balcony. Here is the full story. | thread |
| 4 | Benefits | Why a balcony patch saves more than money | long post |
| 5 | Questions | What do you wish you had known before your first balcony garden? | question post |
| 6 | Steps | From empty balcony to first salad in 5 steps | how-to post |
| 7 | Starter kit | Your first balcony garden for under 50 euros | list post |

The other 23 angles fill the rest of the month. Post one or two a day on the
main network, and adapt the best ones for the second network.
