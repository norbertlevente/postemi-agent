# Postemi for Cursor and Grok Bot

Write, schedule and publish social media posts without leaving the editor:
37 platforms and apps, including Facebook, Instagram, X, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Threads, Bluesky, Pinterest and Mastodon. Postemi previews every post per account (name,
@handle, local publish time, character count) and creates it only after you
confirm.

## What it adds

- **MCP server** `postemi` at `https://mcp.postemi.com/mcp`. Sign in with
  your browser on first use; no key to paste.
- **Skill** `postemi`: the workflow and the rules for posting through Postemi.
- **Skills** `postemi-writing` and `postemi-templates`: how to write posts
  that get read, and the Postemi template and hook library.
- Optional, as its own plugin: **postemi-growth** (how to grow an audience:
  niche, cadence, content plan, numbers). Install it from the same
  marketplace only if you want it.
- **Commands**
  - `/announce-release`: posts about your latest release, from the changelog.
  - `/schedule-post`: one post, at the time you name.

## Requirements

- A Postemi account at [postemi.com](https://postemi.com) with your social channels
  connected, on the Pro plan or higher (Pro, Creator or Team). Basic has no
  API or MCP access.
- Channels are connected in Postemi itself, not from Cursor.

## Try it

- "Announce today's release on LinkedIn and X tomorrow at 9:00."
- "What is scheduled on my social channels this week?"
- "Cancel the LinkedIn post for Friday."
