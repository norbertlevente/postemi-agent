# Hook and headline patterns

25 patterns for a first line, a thread opener, a LinkedIn hook or a title.
Brackets are slots. Each pattern has two made-up examples; write the user's
own version, with their real topic, numbers and experience.

Rules that hold for every pattern:

- The body must deliver what the pattern promises. A list pattern needs the
  full list; a "guaranteed" or "secret" pattern needs a real, specific answer.
- Use real numbers only. "7 mistakes" means seven.
- Cut to the network: long titles for blogs and YouTube, short hooks for X.

## Problems and mistakes

1. **[N] overlooked [things] behind your [problem]**
   - 5 overlooked settings behind your slow laptop
   - 3 overlooked habits behind your afternoon crash
2. **The [N] biggest mistakes people make when [doing something]**
   - The 6 biggest mistakes people make when they open a second shop
   - The 4 biggest mistakes people make when they adopt a puppy
3. **[N] in 10 people [have this problem]. Are you one of them?**
   - 8 in 10 small online shops lose sales at checkout. Is yours one of them?
     (only with a real source for the number)
   - Most new managers skip one-to-ones in their first month. Are you one of
     them?
4. **How to get rid of [problem] for good, even if you have tried everything**
   - How to stop procrastinating on invoices for good, even if you have tried
     everything
   - How to get rid of fruit flies in the kitchen for good, even if you have
     tried everything
5. **How [industry] takes advantage of [audience], and how to fix it**
   - How phone contracts take advantage of older customers, and how to fix it
   - How ticket fees take advantage of fans, and what organisers can do

## How-to and guides

6. **A step-by-step guide to [doing something]**
   - A step-by-step guide to selling your first handmade candle online
   - A step-by-step guide to running your first community meetup
7. **How to [get a result] in [a short time]**
   - How to tidy your inbox to zero in one afternoon
   - How to learn the basics of a spreadsheet in a weekend
8. **How to [get something good] without [giving up something you like]**
   - How to save for a holiday without giving up your weekend coffee
   - How to grow on LinkedIn without posting every day
9. **The best ways to get [result] without needing [usual requirement]**
   - The best ways to get your first clients without a portfolio
   - The best ways to eat well on a budget without meal prepping all Sunday
10. **The complete guide to [topic]**
    - The complete guide to renting your first flat
    - The complete guide to starting a vegetable patch on a balcony
11. **Want [result]? Try [this one thing]**
    - Want more replies to your cold emails? Try this two-line opener
    - Want calmer mornings? Try packing the bag the night before
12. **How to survive your first [big experience]**
    - How to survive your first trade fair as an exhibitor
    - How to survive your first week as a team lead

## Lessons and stories

13. **[N] lessons from [doing something hard or long]**
    - 7 lessons from running a bakery for 10 years
    - 5 lessons from my first 100 customer calls
14. **A day in the life of a [profession]**
    - A day in the life of a hospital night porter
    - A day in the life of a one-person design studio
15. **[Insight], from someone who does it for a living**
    - Why restaurant prices keep rising, from a restaurant owner
    - What makes a CV stand out, from a recruiter who reads 200 a week
16. **What [a credible kind of person] can teach us about [topic]**
    - What an air traffic controller can teach us about focus
    - What a chess coach can teach us about making decisions under pressure
    (use a role, or a real person only when the user has a true, sourced
    lesson from them)
17. **Is [thing] worth it? My honest review**
    - Are online courses worth it? My honest review after 12 of them
    - Is a standing desk worth it? My honest review after a year

## Secrets, surprises and science

18. **The secret to [something people want]**
    - The secret to a sourdough that rises every time
    - The secret to meetings that end on time
19. **The [N] things most people do not know about [topic]**
    - The 7 things most people do not know about buying a used car
    - The 5 things most people do not know about how app stores rank apps
20. **Why [something happens], according to research**
    - Why small talk feels exhausting, according to research
    - Why we remember the end of a holiday most, according to research
21. **[N] things your [detail] says about you**
    - 5 things your email signature says about your business
    - 3 things your shop window says to people walking past
22. **A reliable way to [get or avoid something]**
    - A reliable way to never miss a tax deadline
    - A reliable way to avoid scope creep on client projects
    (stronger words like "guaranteed" only when it truly is)
23. **How to make good money [doing something unexpected]**
    - How a weekend bike repair stall became a full-time income
    - How to earn a living cleaning solar panels

## Lists and resources

24. **The [N] best [books, tools, podcasts] if you want to [goal]**
    - The 5 best books if you want to write clearer emails
    - The 4 best free tools if you run a one-person shop
25. **[N] tips for [getting past an obstacle]**
    - 6 tips for staying active when you work from home
    - 4 tips for asking for a raise when you hate negotiating
