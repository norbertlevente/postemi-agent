# The post log and the weekly review

## The post log

Keep one row per published post. A spreadsheet, a note or a Markdown table in
the user's project is enough; offer to keep it for the user if you can write
files. Fill the planning columns when the post is scheduled and the numbers a
few days after it goes out (LinkedIn posts can keep growing for days).

| Date | Network | Post id | Topic | Angle | Hook pattern | Format | Time | Impressions | Comments | Shares | Saves | Follows | Clicks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-02 | LinkedIn | ... | pricing | Lessons | honest admission | long post + image | 08:30 | 1,840 | 14 | 3 | 9 | 6 | 11 |

- **Post id:** from `create_post`, `list_posts` or the CLI, so the numbers can
  be matched later (`postemi analytics:post <post-id>`).
- **Angle:** how-to, analysis, inspiration, human nature, or one of the 30
  angles.
- **Hook pattern:** which opener or pattern from `postemi-writing` or which
  template from `postemi-templates`.
- Leave a number empty rather than guessing it.

## Getting the numbers

- Postemi Insights in the web app, per channel and per period.
- CLI: `postemi analytics:post <post-id> -d 7` per post;
  `postemi analytics:platform <integration-id> -d 30` per channel.
- MCP clients: ask the user for the numbers from Insights or the network's
  own analytics (there is no analytics tool on MCP).

## The weekly review (15 minutes)

1. **Fill the log** for last week's posts.
2. **Rank** by comments plus shares plus saves (or engagement rate when reach
   differs a lot). Look at the top three and the bottom three.
3. **Find the pattern.** What do the winners share: topic, angle, hook,
   format, length, time, media? What do the losers share?
4. **One decision.** Pick one bigger change for next week and write it down:
   "two more posts on pricing lessons", "carousels instead of text on
   LinkedIn", "move X posts to the evening". Change one main thing at a time,
   so next week's numbers can show whether it helped.
5. **Plan and schedule** next week with that change, in Postemi.
6. **Reuse the winner:** schedule an adapted version of the best post on the
   second network, or a follow-up post that goes deeper.

## The monthly review

- Followers and reach per network over the month (Insights): growing, flat,
  falling?
- The niche: which topic and angle won most weeks? Go deeper there.
- The profile link: are people clicking and signing up? If not, change the
  offer or the bio before posting more.
- Cadence: could the user keep it? Lower it rather than skip weeks.
- Networks: keep the main one; add, keep or drop the second one.

## Reading small numbers

- Compare posts with each other, not with other accounts.
- A post with two thoughtful comments beat a post with ten likes and no
  comments.
- Wait for at least two or three posts of the same kind before calling a
  pattern; one post can be luck.
- When everything is low, change something big (topic, format, network), not
  a word in the hook.
